# BSides Delaware 2026

**Workshop Title:** "Do you know what is hOT right now?"

## Abstract:
Operational technology (OT) is becoming a much important field of cybersecurity as people and companies start to develop a better understanding of the risks from these critical systems. OT is embedded in nearly every industry in some way and knowing what these systems are and how you can interact with them can better help you to defend them. In this workshop, participants will learn about different types of OT equipment and systems, learn about some of the previous OT-related cyber incidents, and interact with a virtual OT environment.

## Why is it Important?
The field of OT cyber security is growing and people are needed to join in the fight to keep the lights on, water clean, and planes flying.
